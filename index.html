Questions collectors usually ask

Does Spindle change my Discogs collection?

Spindle does not add or remove records from your Discogs collection. However, when syncing shelf layouts and genre changes between devices, Spindle may update collection notes associated with your records.

Do I need a Discogs account?

No. You can import a CSV or JSON file and work fully offline. Discogs sign-in is optional when you want live sync and marketplace prices.

Can I use Spindle without Discogs?

Yes. Catalog without Discogs on the phone, or import a spreadsheet. Spindle exports work as-is.

Does it work with large collections?

Free covers up to 100 records. Pro unlocks unlimited size for bigger cabinets.

Does one Pro purchase cover Windows and Android?

Yes. One $24 key unlocks both. No second subscription.

Where is my collection data stored?

On your computer and phone. Spindle does not run a cloud account for your collection. See the privacy policy for details.

How does Windows ↔ Android sync work?

Your cabinets, shelf layout, and genre edits stay aligned between apps. Spindle can use Discogs collection notes as the bridge so you do not need a Spindle server — details are in the app docs when you connect Discogs.
